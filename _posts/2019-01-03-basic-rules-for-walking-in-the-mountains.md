This project is an infographic that I was supposed to create, the infographic is about one of the most infamous data breaches in history.
For this infographic I had to use my research skills to find credible data and then use my creativity to come up with an infographic that presents this information effectively. 
What I learned from this was that an MFA is very important, and that it’s crucial to have it if you want everything to be fully secure. 
A challenge I had to overcome was positioning the images on the doc, it wouldn't fit right on the page and I spent a good 20 minutes trying to fix it until I eventually found out how to position it right for an infographic on a google doc.
