This Project is supposed to represent the IT team inside of Richland High School, I was supposed to create a diagram that shows the flow of highest role to lowest inside the team.
One of the most important skills I used was my creative thinking. I came up with the background as well as color coordinating everything to match with the school's colors. I used canva which is an online website and app that allows users to create projects, that being posters to custom merchandise to interactive websites.
Something I learned was actually how many more roles are in an IT team for a school.
A challenge I had to solve was my explanation for why I made the diagram the way it is. I have a hard time coming up with statements sometimes but I overcame it by pushing through and really thinking about what I want to say and explaining it right.
