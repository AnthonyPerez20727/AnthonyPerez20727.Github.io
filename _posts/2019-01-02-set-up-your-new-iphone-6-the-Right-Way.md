This project is a diagram that I was tasked to create. This diagram is supposed to represent the flow of an Admin to Students to Teachers, to switch to a server in a school. 
The most important skill I used was my creative thinking, having to come up with which shape to represent the person or item, making sure the flow of the arrows are correct and not in the wrong direction. 
What I learned was to create a nice simple diagram with just shapes and simple titles without having to provide information and an explanation, just a drawing to represent. 
The biggest challenge I had was being indecisive on which shapes to choose to represent my data.
